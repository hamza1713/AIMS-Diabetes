# AIMS Diabetes — User Guide

This guide walks through every screen of the AIMS Diabetes app: what it shows, and how to use it.

> **Important.** AIMS Diabetes helps you record and understand your health data. It is not a medical device and does not give diagnoses. Always follow your clinician's advice, and seek urgent care for very high or very low glucose with symptoms.

## 1. Getting started

### Create an account

1. Open the app and tap **Sign up** at the bottom of the sign-in screen.
2. Enter your **full name**, **email** and a **password** (at least 8 characters), and confirm the password.
3. Tick **I agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy** and tap **Create account**.

You are signed in straight away and land on **Home**.

### Sign in

Enter your email and password and tap **Sign in**. The app keeps you signed in securely on this device until you log out.

### Forgot your password

On the sign-in screen tap **Forgot password?**, enter your email and tap **Send reset link**.

### Complete your profile (recommended)

Go to **Profile → Edit profile** and add your age, gender, height, weight and diabetes type. This lets the app show your BMI and pre-fill the risk test.

## 2. Finding your way around

The bar at the bottom of the screen has four tabs and a round **+** button:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your day at a glance. |
| **Chat** | Ask the AI Assistant about diabetes. |
| **+** | Quick add: **Glucose**, **Meal**, **Workout**, **Water** or **Medication**. |
| **Track** | All your logs and the individual trackers. |
| **Profile** | Your details, reports, settings and log out. |

Tip: pull down on most screens to refresh them.

## 3. Home

| Section | Details |
|---|---|
| **Header** | Greeting and your name. The **bell** opens Notifications; a badge shows unread ones. |
| **Latest glucose** | Your most recent reading, when you took it, a badge (**Low** / **In range** / **High**) and a 7-day trend line. |
| **Quick actions** | One-tap **Glucose**, **Meal**, **Water** and **Reports**. |
| **Today** | Water drunk vs your goal, active minutes vs goal, and medication doses taken. Tap a tile to open that tracker. |
| **Reminders** | Medication doses still due today. |
| **Diabetes risk** | Your last risk-test result, or a prompt to take the test. |

**In range** means 70–180 mg/dL, the commonly used target. Your clinician may set different targets for you.

## 4. AI Assistant

Ask questions such as *"What is a normal fasting glucose?"* or *"How does metformin work?"*.

- Type in the box at the bottom and tap the **↑** button, or tap a suggested question.
- While the assistant works you'll see what it's doing (e.g. *Analyzing query intent…*, *Synthesising answer…*). The answer then appears word by word.
- Tap **■ (stop)** to stop an answer early.
- Answers come from clinical research papers. **Source cards** under an answer show the paper, journal, year and page. Tap one to see the original page.
- Tap **↻** in the top bar to start a new conversation (this clears the chat history).

The assistant gives general information, not personal medical advice.

## 5. Health tracking

Open the **Track** tab to see:

- A **week strip** — tap a day to see everything logged that day.
- **Tracker tiles** — Glucose, Meals, Exercise, Water, Medications and Risk test.
- **The day's log** — all entries, newest first.

### 5.1 Glucose

**Add a reading:** **+ → Glucose**, or **Add reading** on the Glucose screen.

1. Type the value in mg/dL (20–600).
2. Choose **When**: *Fasting*, *Before meal*, *After meal*, *Bedtime* or *Random*.
3. Change the date or time if you're logging an earlier reading.
4. Optionally add a note (e.g. "after a walk"), then tap **Save reading**.

**The Glucose screen shows:**

- Your current reading, colour-coded: green = in range, amber = low, red = high.
- A 7-day chart with the shaded 70–180 target band.
- **7-day average**, **% in range** and **readings per day**.
- Your recent readings.

### 5.2 Meals

**Add a meal:** **+ → Meal**.

1. Pick the **meal type** (it's pre-selected from the time of day).
2. Enter the **food** name and **carbs (g)**; calories, protein and fat are optional.
3. Adjust the time if needed and tap **Save meal**.

The **Meals** screen shows today's calories against your goal, carbs/protein/fat bars, and today's meals.

### 5.3 Vision AI meal scan

Let the camera estimate your meal's nutrition:

1. **Meals → camera icon**, or **Add Meal → Scan with camera**.
2. Take a photo (**Camera**) or choose one (**Gallery**).
3. The app shows the recognised food, how sure it is, carbs, calories, protein, fat and glycemic load, plus a short tip.
4. Tap **Add to log** — the Add Meal form opens pre-filled. Check the numbers, adjust if needed, and save.

Photo estimates are approximate; portion size is the hardest part to judge from a picture.

### 5.4 Exercise

**Log a workout:** **+ → Workout**. Pick the activity, enter minutes (and calories if you know them), choose the intensity and save.

The **Exercise** screen shows today's active minutes vs your daily goal, this week's total and active days, a 7-day bar chart, and today's workouts.

### 5.5 Water

- On the **Water** screen, tap **+250 ml**, **+500 ml** or **+750 ml** to log instantly.
- Tap **Custom amount** (or **+ → Water**) to log a different amount or time.

The ring fills toward your daily goal (2.0 L by default).

### 5.6 Medications

**Add a medication:** **+ → Medication**.

1. Enter the **name** and **dose** (e.g. *Metformin*, *500 mg*).
2. Choose the **form**: tablet, capsule, injection or liquid.
3. Choose **times per day** (1×, 2× or 3×) and tap each time chip to set the exact time.
4. Tap **Save medication**.

**The Medications screen shows:**

- **Weekly adherence** — bars for Monday to Sunday, showing how many scheduled doses you took.
- **Today's schedule** — tick a dose when you take it. Ticked doses count toward adherence and drop off Home's reminders.
- **My medications** — tap the bin icon to remove one.

Medication reminders are switched on or off for all medications in **Settings → Medication reminders**.

## 6. Diabetes risk test

This is the CDC / American Diabetes Association **prediabetes risk test**: 7 questions that take about a minute.

1. Open it from **Track → Risk test**, the **Diabetes risk** card on Home, or **Profile → Risk assessment**.
2. Tap **Start test**, then answer one question per screen. The bar at the top shows your progress, and **←** goes back.
   - Age, sex, gestational diabetes (women only), family history, high blood pressure, physical activity, height and weight.
   - If your profile already has your age, sex, height and weight, they're filled in for you.
3. Tap **See my result**.

**Your result:**

- **Score out of 11** and a band: **Low**, **Moderate** or **High risk**. A score of **5 or more** means you may have prediabetes or be at high risk for type 2 diabetes.
- **What contributed** — the points from each answer.
- **Recommendations** — practical next steps.

Results are saved to your account; Home shows the latest. If you're offline, you still see your result, but it's marked *Not saved*. **Retake** starts the test again.

This test is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Talk to your doctor about a blood test (HbA1c or fasting glucose) if your score is high.

## 7. Analytics & reports

Open from **Home → Reports** or **Profile → Analytics & reports**. Use **‹ ›** to change month.

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Avg glucose** | Average of all readings that month. |
| **Time in range** | % of readings between 70 and 180 mg/dL. |
| **Avg carbs / day** | Average carbs on days you logged meals. |
| **Active days** | Days with at least one workout. |
| **Estimated HbA1c** | An estimate from your average glucose. It is not a lab result. |
| **Glucose trend** | Daily average glucose across the month, with the target band shaded. |

*Download PDF report* is coming in a future update.

## 8. Profile

The Profile tab shows your name, email, age, height, weight and **BMI**, plus links to:

- **Edit profile** — name, gender, age, height, weight and diabetes type (Type 1, Type 2, Prediabetes, Gestational or None). Your email can't be changed here.
- **Analytics & reports**, **Risk assessment**, **Notifications**, **Settings**.
- **Log out**.

## 9. Settings

| Section | Options |
|---|---|
| **Account** | Edit profile; **Change password** (enter your current and a new password). |
| **Preferences** | **Glucose units** (mg/dL or mmol/L), **Language** (English, Urdu), **Theme** (System, Light, Dark). |
| **Notifications** | **Medication reminders**, **High / low glucose alerts**, **Daily health tips**. |
| **Support** | Help and app version, privacy information. |
| **Log out** | Signs you out of this device. |

Settings save as soon as you change them. If saving fails (for example, you're offline), the switch flips back and a message explains why.

## 10. Notifications

The bell on Home opens your notifications, grouped into **Today** and **Earlier**. They include:

- medication reminders,
- glucose alerts,
- confirmations (e.g. a finished risk test),
- tips.

Unread items are highlighted with a dot; tap **Mark all read** to clear them.

## 11. Offline, errors and loading

| You see | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Grey placeholder cards | Data is loading. |
| Red **No connection** bar | Your device is offline. Logging needs a connection. |
| **Something went wrong** + **Try again** | The server couldn't be reached or returned an error. Check your connection and tap **Try again**. |
| An illustration with a button (e.g. *No readings yet*) | There's nothing logged yet. The button takes you to add your first entry. |
| You're back on the sign-in screen | Your session expired. Sign in again. |

## 12. Privacy & your data

- Your health data is stored on your account on the AIMS server and is only visible to you.
- Your sign-in is stored securely on the device (Keychain / Keystore). **Log out** removes it.
- Meal photos are sent only for analysis when you use Vision AI.
- AI Assistant conversations are saved so you can see your history; **↻ New chat** clears them.

## 13. FAQ

**What does "in range" mean?**
70–180 mg/dL, a widely used target. Your clinician may give you different targets.

**Can I log something I forgot earlier?**
Yes. Every add form has a date and time — change them before saving.

**Why doesn't the AI Assistant answer my question?**
It only answers from the research papers your organisation has loaded. If none match, it says so.

**Is the Vision AI nutrition exact?**
No — it's an estimate. Check it against the packaging or your usual portions before saving.

**I switched phones. Is my data still there?**
Yes. Sign in with the same email and your history loads from the server.
